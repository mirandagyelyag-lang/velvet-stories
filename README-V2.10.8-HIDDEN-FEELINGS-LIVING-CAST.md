# Velvet Stories v2.10.8 · Hidden Feelings & Living Cast

- Characters may feel intensely in private while behaving proportionately in public.
- Romantic attention no longer forces obsession or constant pursuit.
- Meaningful physical tells remain welcome, but repetition without new information is discouraged.
- Named secondary characters gain durable cast continuity and remain active until they leave.
- Flirting can continue naturally when personality and scene support it.
- Sarcasm, rhetorical questions, polished comeback dialogue and argument-winning invention are strongly limited.
- Adds structured `cast_updates` to remember secondary-character relationships, personality notes, knowledge and latest meaningful interaction.
