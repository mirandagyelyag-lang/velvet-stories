# Velvet Stories v2.6.12 · Calm Ambience Rebuild

This release replaces the harsh procedural ambience beds that could sound like TV static on phones.

- Rain now uses a bundled soft rain recording-style loop with rounded droplets and no high-frequency white-noise bed.
- Night City and the legacy Street Racing mode are one environment: dark city traffic plus occasional distant engine passes.
- Café uses a quiet room tone, muffled speech-shaped moments, ceramic clinks and very light steam instead of a constant hiss.
- Fireplace is quieter in the room bed and focuses on flame body and wood crackles.
- Home · TV is a quiet room with distant muffled television speech and no brown-noise wind layer.
- Existing stories saved as `street_racing` remain compatible and transparently play Night City · racing.
- Bundled ambience MP3 files are included in the PWA precache for consistent playback across Android and desktop.
