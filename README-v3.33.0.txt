VELVET STORIES v3.33.0 — HUMAN TURN-TAKING + CONVERSATION RHYTHM

This release makes conversations behave less like alternating AI turns and more like human exchanges.

Highlights
- Turn-taking engine: micro replies, delayed answers, partial answers, topic drops/returns, interruption repair and silence tolerance.
- Conversation Memory Threads: unfinished questions, awkward subjects, interrupted confessions, private jokes, unresolved arguments and practical plans can remain pending without being repeated every turn.
- Character-specific conversational dominance, topic stamina and silence tolerance.
- Group traffic control: only relevant speakers answer; overlap/interruption is allowed when the scene supports it.
- Answer-before-flourish and no compulsory follow-up question.
- Short input -> short output, gesture-only turns and scene stillness are explicitly supported.
- Deterministic guards for compulsive question endings, forced topic shifts, micro-turn padding and overcrowded group replies.
- Preserves v3.32 POV privacy and perception/knowledge realism.
