The world now remembers who each character is outside the protagonist.

- Public identity and reputation are treated as hard creator canon.
- Domain-scoped recognition: campus, underground racing, wealth/status, athletics, public fame and authority.
- Famous / feared / respected / desired characters create subtle world reactions instead of social anonymity.
- Outside admirers, acquaintances, rivals, teammates, fans, staff and status-seekers can approach independently.
- Romantic focus no longer deletes outside attention.
- Admirers are not instantly erased just to protect the ship.
- Ongoing work / sport / racing / business / social life survives off-screen and can re-enter after time gaps or life questions.
- Existing Grounded Reality rules still forbid fabricated named professors, schedules, races, captains or NPC history.
- One small social footprint is enough; Velvet should not turn every public scene into a crowd spectacle.
