# Velvet Stories v2.10.24 — Emotional Responsiveness

Clear emotional cues now require a meaningful, character-specific response instead of passive atmospheric observation.

- Tears, fear, shaking, anger, hurt, or visible distress must change the beat.
- Quiet characters may stay quiet, but they still make a concrete choice or action.
- No forced touch, therapy language, or instant vulnerability.
- Narrative Doctor repairs passive emotional freezing via `emotional_cue_passivity`.
