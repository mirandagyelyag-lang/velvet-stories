# Velvet Stories v2.11.20 — Implicit Time Skip

Time-skip messages are director-state instructions, not dialogue prompts.

- `Time skip 3 months later, we were nicer to each other` is recognized as a time skip.
- The elapsed time and relationship shift become silent canon.
- The reply lands directly inside a normal post-skip scene and shows the new dynamic through behavior.
- Characters do not comment on, count, summarize, or congratulate the time jump.
- Exposition such as “a whole three months of basic civility” is rejected and repaired.
- v2.11.19 hedged live-reply behavior and all prior continuity/pursuit guards remain intact.
