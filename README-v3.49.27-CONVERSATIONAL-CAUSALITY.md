# Velvet Stories v3.49.27

Conversational Causality + Direct Answer Lock.

- Direct WHY questions resolve to the specific prior action and its grounded trigger.
- Characters may conceal or deflect motives, but cannot invent unrelated witty excuses.
- Generic acknowledgements such as “Okay.” are rejected for direct causal questions.
- Dead banter / lexical echoes do not resurrect old joke words after the conversation advances.
- Personality controls how the answer sounds, not whether every line needs a punchline.
