VELVET STORIES v3.27.0 — AUTONOMOUS LIFE + CONSEQUENCE ENGINE

This release builds on Character DNA 2.0. Characters no longer just sound different; they carry different lives, obligations, expectations, mistakes, consequences, arcs and off-screen relationships.

NEW SYSTEMS
1. Autonomous Character Engine 3.0
   - Persistent agenda, outside obligation, private goal and time pressure.
   - Characters may be busy, leave, postpone, choose friends/work/family, or say no without it automatically meaning rejection.

2. Consequence Engine 3.0
   - Conflict, promises, public choices and practical fallout persist across scenes.
   - A new scene or apology no longer resets trust/access/tone automatically.

3. Scene Rhythm Engine 3.0
   - OPEN / DEVELOP / TURN / LAND / CLOSE.
   - Scenes are allowed to end naturally instead of looping questions forever.

4. Selective Memory 3.0
   - High-salience memories drive behavior.
   - Routine trivia is filtered from automatic memory unless it becomes meaningful.

5. Relationship Expectations 3.0
   - Each character forms their own expectations about contact, closeness, conflict and repair.
   - Expectations are subjective and never invent the user's feelings.

6. Human Imperfection 3.0
   - Character-specific bad timing, defensiveness, incomplete answers, minor forgetfulness and plausible misunderstandings.

7. NPC Autonomy 3.0
   - Supporting characters keep goals, loyalties and off-screen motion unrelated to the protagonist.

8. Romance Progression 3.0
   - Relationship phases require earned evidence and changed expectations.
   - No instant jump from tension to devotion, no replayed firsts, no forced romance.

9. Long-Term Character Arcs 3.0
   - Durable growth requires repeated evidence.
   - Major canon beats can seed an ongoing story arc; stress can trigger partial relapse without resetting growth.

10. Character Clone Lab
   - Diagnostics now includes a visible blind test.
   - Choose 2-5 saved characters, give them the exact same user line, and Velvet scores whether the underlying reaction logic is actually distinct.
