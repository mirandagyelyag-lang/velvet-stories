VELVET STORIES v3.13.11 — PULSE CONTINUE CENTER

Pulse is now the quickest place to decide what to continue and reopen the exact story.

- Every character gets a shelf, including a welcoming empty state before their first story.
- Each shelf chooses one clear Continue now story, prioritizing unfinished threads.
- Remaining stories stay organized as Unfinished, Recent and Waiting for you.
- Quick actions open a new story, the character's Memories, their Profile or all Stories.
- Group Stories stay in their own shelves and never mix with individual character stories.
- Story titles and return-point copy are guarded against null or serialized null values.
- The v3.13.10 single native scroll remains unchanged; Pulse adds no nested scroll owner.
