Velvet Stories v3.49.15 · Story Library · Memory Experience · Data Safety

Story Library
- Custom story collections with safe removal: removing a shelf never deletes stories.
- Deep Story Library search across older messages and memories.
- Global search now includes archived stories and a much deeper history window.
- Recovery Vault beside Favorites, Archived and Trash.

Memory Experience
- Character Memory Book now has Book, Timeline and Us views.
- Timeline merges memories, story milestones and story timeline beats.
- Relationship Journal surfaces human-readable changes, promises, boundaries, conflict/repair, inside jokes and meaningful places without exposing numeric affection meters.

Data Safety
- IndexedDB Safety Vault stores up to five local account snapshots for 30 days.
- One quiet account snapshot is refreshed automatically each day after sign-in.
- Permanent story deletion requires a successful local recovery snapshot first.
- Recovery story copies are retained for 30 days and can be restored from the Story Library.
- Manual full-backup downloads and restore remain available in Settings.
- Safety Vault local-state capture is whitelist-only and excludes auth/session tokens.
