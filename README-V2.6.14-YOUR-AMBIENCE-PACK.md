# Velvet Stories v2.6.14 · Your Ambience Pack

This release replaces the v2.6.13 ambience placeholders with the eight audio clips selected/generated by the user.

## Included ambience
- Rain
- Night
- Street Racing
- Café
- Campus
- Fireplace
- Home · TV
- Party

## Audio handling
- WAV uploads were used as the mastering source.
- App assets are encoded as stereo 48 kHz MP3 at 192 kbps for broad browser/PWA compatibility.
- Loudness is gently normalized per room so Party/Street Racing do not overwhelm the chat and Home TV remains audible.
- No WebAudio white/pink/brown-noise ambience generators are used.
- Night and Street Racing remain separate modes.

The extra `natural_recording_fe...` upload was intentionally not mapped because Velvet has eight defined ambience categories and it was not tied to one of them.
