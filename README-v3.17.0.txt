Velvet Stories v3.17.0 — Living Character Engine

Character behavior:
- Human imperfection: correction/conflict no longer turns characters instantly emotionally fluent.
- Asymmetric growth: trust, affection, apology skill, pride, jealousy and vulnerability can develop at different speeds.
- Relapse without reset: old defenses can reappear under stress without erasing earned development.
- Learning is not optimization: characters retain disagreement, incompatible wants, obligations and imperfect memory.
- Subtext gets room: less self-explanation, more behavior/timing/omissions.
- Relationship phases are descriptive, not a forced romance ladder.

World and supporting cast:
- Supporting cast can now return role + goals in the structured response and persist them.
- NPC continuity is explicitly allowed off-screen and independently of the protagonist.
- Independent life can compete with romance without being treated as rejection.

Story dynamics:
- Initiative contradiction fixed: a real spoken decision/invitation/refusal/reveal can count as initiative.
- Arc progress is based on recorded meaningful timeline events, not keyword hits.
- Conflict repair no longer begins from a pretty apology alone; it needs a concrete repair or grounded repair event.
- Forgotten knowledge maps to unknown rather than accidentally becoming known again.
