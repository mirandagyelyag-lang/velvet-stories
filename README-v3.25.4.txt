VELVET STORIES v3.25.4 — VOICE IDENTITY

Goal: stop characters from collapsing into the same polished, witty, self-aware AI voice.

Changes:
- Blind Voice Test on every roleplay draft.
- Plain-question rule for mundane conversation.
- Character-specific social tactic instead of universal witty/sarcastic banter.
- Explicit ban on generic college filler (GPA, mid-semester burnout, X% brain capacity, lab reports, escaping the library, caffeine survival).
- Runtime detector rejects that cadence and spends the single repair pass on a more character-specific rewrite.
- Existing canon, POV ownership, memory, relationship, PWA and mobile protections remain intact.
