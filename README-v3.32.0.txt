VELVET STORIES v3.32.0 — PERCEPTION & KNOWLEDGE REALISM

Goal: characters react only to what they could plausibly see, hear, know, remember, infer or be told.

Included
- Perception Reality contract: observation is separated from interpretation.
- POV Privacy Lock 2.0: private asterisk narration stays inaccessible.
- Nonverbal Ambiguity Guard: a smile, silence, crossed arms or looking away never reveals a motive by itself.
- Character-specific knowledge ledger: known / suspected / rumor / forgotten stay distinct.
- Secret firewall: secret rows owned by another character are not exposed in the model turn contract.
- Information propagation realism: off-screen knowledge requires a plausible witness/message/source.
- Presence / hearing rules: in-person characters cannot hear a turn from another room or while absent unless canon establishes it.
- Public vs private social context remains character-specific.
- Response Weight Matching: tiny mundane turns do not receive giant interpretive speeches.
- Reality Judge: final self-check covers perception, epistemic status, secrets, nonverbal ambiguity, causality and response weight.
- Deterministic regression guards for mind-reading, secret leakage, certainty from ambiguous gestures and rumor->fact collapse.

The user still owns all inner thoughts, motives, feelings, body choices and unstaged movement.
