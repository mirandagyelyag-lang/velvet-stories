# Velvet Stories v3.49.32 — Human Social Intelligence

Adds a social cognition layer above Human Cognition Pipeline and Individual Human Psyche: read-the-room inference, public/private behavior, face-saving, audience geometry, third-party knowledge boundaries, indirect language, group dynamics, social cost, calibrated humor, anti-romance projection, status realism, and socially plausible non-disclosure.

The goal is not perfect social intelligence. Characters may misread ambiguous cues, recover late, protect face badly, or choose silence, while never gaining omniscient access to other minds.
