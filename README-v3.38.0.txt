Long-Term Personality Evolution + Character Growth

Same person, different history.

- Core temperament, values, voice and social identity remain anchored.
- Defenses and learned behavior change only through repeated grounded evidence.
- Relationship-specific growth can stay relationship-specific.
- Behavioral milestones are remembered without turning every warm line into a turning point.
- Regression under stress can revive an old defense without erasing proven growth.
- Beliefs can be challenged gradually rather than flipped.
- Off-screen growth requires an established life/domain/arc cause.
- Romance never replaces a difficult, guarded, intimidating or sarcastic character with a generic softer personality.
- Character Evolution Lab compares chapter-one behavior with earned later behavior.
